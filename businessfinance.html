MVP limitations

This prototype is designed for straightforward small-business bookkeeping. Taxes, inventory costing methods, payroll liabilities, sales tax, complex accruals, multi-currency activity, and bank reconciliation are not fully automated. Use an accountant before relying on the output for tax filings or external reporting.
